This walks through one page from start to finish: create it, add blocks, check it on different screen sizes, and publish it.

<Steps>

<Step title="Sign in">

Open your site's address and sign in with the admin email and password you chose in the setup wizard.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose Page">

Press **New** at the top of the admin. In the **What are you making?** chooser, pick **Page**. Give the page a title. Its address comes from the title, and you can change the address later in **Page settings**.

Under **Start from**, pick one of your templates, or leave it blank to start empty. If the AI assistant is set up, you can also write a short description. The assistant uses it to build the page.

</Step>

<Step title="Add blocks">

The page builder opens with the **Blocks** panel. The blocks are grouped, and you can drag any block onto the page. Headings, text, images, buttons, columns, grids and forms are all blocks.

</Step>

<Step title="Style the blocks">

Select a block to see its settings. The settings are split into **Content**, **Style** and **Advanced**. Your changes show on the page as you make them.

</Step>

<Step title="Check each screen size">

Use the device preview to switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile**. Check that the page reads well at each size.

</Step>

<Step title="Preview and publish">

Press **Preview** to see the page as visitors will see it. Press **Publish** to make the page live at its address.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Your page is live">

Open the page's address in a new browser tab to see it as a visitor does.

</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Undo a mistake">

Press `Ctrl` + `Z` to undo. On a Mac, press `⌘` + `Z`.

</Callout>
