A blog is a list of posts on your site. Each post belongs to a blog. Create a blog before you write your first post.

## Write a post

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose Blog post">

Press **New** at the top of the admin. In the **What are you making?** chooser, pick **Blog post**. The card says it opens the post editor, and that the look of the blog is set once.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the post">

Write the post in the post editor, then save it.

</Step>

<Step title="Publish when it is ready">

A new post is a draft. It is not on your site until you publish it.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Post blocks

Post blocks show post content on any page. The Post group of the Blocks panel has blocks such as:

- **Post Title** and **Featured Image**, for the title and picture of a post.
- **Excerpt**, for a short summary.
- **Post List**, for a list of posts from a blog.
- **Comments**, for the comments on a post.
- **TOC** and **Progress**, for a table of contents and reading progress.

## Comments

Subscribers can comment. Editors and admins approve, mark as spam, change or delete comments. Read more about roles in [People and roles](/docs/people-and-roles).

<Callout type="tip" title="Import posts from WordPress">

If your posts live on a WordPress site, you can bring them over. See [Import from WordPress](/docs/pages-and-content/wordpress-import).

</Callout>
